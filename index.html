Welcome to Family Forest

Your personal family history, on your device. Private, offline, and entirely yours.

What is Family Forest?

Family Forest is a personal family tree app. There is no account to create, no subscription, and your data is never sent anywhere. Everything you enter stays on your phone or computer.

Rather than showing your entire family as one overwhelming diagram, it centres on one person at a time — with their immediate family around them. Tap anyone to move to their perspective.

Installing on iPhone

  1. Open Safari and navigate to the app's web address
  2. Tap the Share button (box with an arrow) at the bottom of the screen
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add

The app works fully offline once installed.

Adding Your First Person

Tap + Add First Person to begin — start with yourself. You will be asked for a name, role label, and a few optional details. Nothing is required. You can fill in more at any time.

Use the Role / Label field freely — labels like Me, Maternal grandmother, or Possible great-uncle are for your own navigation and can be changed any time.

Uncertain and Disputed Relationships

Family history is full of uncertainty. Any relationship can be marked as uncertain or disputed — shown in gold. To toggle it, tap a person on the canvas and use the ⚑ button in the action bar.

A person's identity can also be marked as uncertain separately — meaning you know someone of that description existed, but are not sure exactly who they were.

Mark uncertainties as you go. It is far better to flag something as uncertain now than to forget later that a connection was speculative.

Person Detail Panel

Tap Edit on any person to open their full record. The panel has five tabs.

Vitals

Core identity: name, maiden name, role, birth, death, burial, phone, and general notes. Also includes the profile photo, deceased toggle, uncertainty flags, and tags. Buttons at the bottom let you view the tree from this person's perspective, print their record, or delete them.

Life Events

Structured entries for residences, occupations, marriages and unions (including a separate anniversary date), and education.

Relationships

A full list of connections — parents, partners, children, siblings. Add new people, link existing ones, toggle the uncertain flag on any relationship, or remove links.

Notes

Anecdotes and stories. Free-form entries with a title and optional date. Use this for family memories, legends, things you were told — anything that gives texture to the record beyond dates and places.

Documents

Photos and images (any can be set as the profile photo), plus document references — scanned certificates, archive references, GRO index numbers, source notes.

The Index

The Index tab shows an alphabetical list of everyone in your tree. Search by name, maiden name, role, birthplace, notes, or tags. Tap anyone to jump to their tree and open their record.

Tags

Add searchable labels to any person from the Vitals tab — for example: WWI service, emigrated, needs death certificate, possible illegitimate birth. Tags make it easy to filter and return to specific threads of research.

Printing

Individual record: open the Vitals tab and tap Print Record. Produces a formatted family record with photo, vital dates, connections, life events, notes, and sources.

Full directory: go to the Data tab and tap Print Full Directory for an alphabetical table of everyone in the tree.

Exporting and Backing Up

Go to the Data tab and tap Export Family Tree (JSON) to download a complete backup. To restore from a backup, use Import JSON File — note this replaces all current data.

Export regularly. Your data lives in your browser's local storage. Clearing Safari's website data on iPhone will delete your tree. Export after any significant session.

Importing from Ancestry or MyHeritage

Export a GEDCOM file (.ged) from your existing genealogy platform and import it via the Data tab. People are added to your existing tree — nothing is replaced. Names, dates, places, occupations, residences, and marriage information are all imported.

To export from Ancestry: go to your tree → Tree Settings → Export Tree.

Your Data and Privacy

Everything in Family Forest lives on your device. Your tree is stored in your browser's local storage — it does not leave your phone or computer unless you explicitly export it. There is no account, no server, and no cloud backup by default.
